# Manufacturing ERP Pro: Sales & Marketing Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
Manufacturing ERP Pro is positioned as the "WordPress of Manufacturing"—the only system that gives SMEs the power of an enterprise ERP (like Protean or SAP) with the simplicity and cost-effectiveness of the world's most popular CMS.

## 2. Target Personas

### Persona A: "Growth-Stuck" Gary (The Founder/Owner)
- **Role:** Owner of a small leather goods or apparel company (10-30 employees).
- **Pain Points:**
    - Losing money on raw material waste.
    - Doesn't know his exact profit margin per SKU.
    - Stressed by "spreadsheet chaos."
- **Goals:** Scale production without hiring 5 more office staff.
- **Buying Trigger:** A stock-out that delayed a major order or a quality defect that ruined a relationship.

### Persona B: "Manual" Mary (The Operations Manager)
- **Role:** Head of Production in a mid-sized furniture or electronics assembly plant.
- **Pain Points:**
    - Spends 3 hours a day updating Excel files.
    - Can't tell the owner when an order will be finished.
    - Tired of "shouting across the floor" to find status.
- **Goals:** Real-time visibility and a "single source of truth."
- **Buying Trigger:** The realization that her current "system" (spreadsheets) will break if they add 5 more workers.

### Persona C: "Tech-Forward" Tim (The WordPress Agency Owner)
- **Role:** Running a niche agency serving B2B manufacturing clients.
- **Pain Points:**
    - Clients asking for inventory solutions that "just work" with their site.
    - High cost of custom-building ERP features.
- **Goals:** Offer a high-value, recurring-revenue service to his manufacturing clients.
- **Buying Trigger:** A client asking, "Can we manage our production floor inside our website?"

## 3. Marketing Strategy: The "Trojan Horse" Method

### Step 1: Content as Authority
Publish deep-dives on "The Real Cost of a 5% Yield Loss" and "Why Excel is Killing Your Factory." Position the ERP not as a tool, but as a **profit recovery system**.

### Step 2: The Free "Inventory Health Audit"
Offer a free diagnostic tool (or simple spreadsheet template) that calculates a company's "Efficiency Leak." This builds trust and captures leads.

### Step 3: Industry-Specific "Starter Kits"
Create pre-built configurations for Leather, Apparel, Furniture, and Electronics. People buy solutions for *their* specific problem, not general software.

## 4. Sales Strategy

### Low-Friction Entry
Offer a "14-Day Production Trial" with the **LeatherCraft Co.** sample data pre-loaded. Let them see their "dream factory" in 60 seconds.

### The "Aha!" Demo
The Visual BOM Builder is the primary sales tool. Showing how a 5-level assembly can be built and costed by dragging and dropping is the "magic moment" that closes the deal.

### Channel Partnerships
Partner with WordPress Hosting companies (like WP Engine or Kinsta) to offer a "Manufacturing Stack"—pre-optimized environments for high-performance ERP database operations.

## 5. Pricing Strategy (The "Margin Recovery" Model)
- **Starter ($149/mo):** For Gary. 1 Warehouse, basic BOM, Inventory. Paid for by saving 2 hours of admin/week.
- **Pro ($399/mo):** For Mary. Unlimited Warehouses, MRP, Quality, Capacity Planning. Paid for by reducing material waste by 10%.
- **Enterprise ($999/mo+):** For scaling plants. Custom API hooks, Priority Support, On-site training.
