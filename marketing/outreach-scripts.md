**Target:** Operations Managers or Owners.
**Script:**
"Hi [Name], I noticed you're scaling up [Company Name]'s production. I recently built a visual tool for leather/apparel factories that helps cut material waste by 15% using drag-and-drop BOMs inside WordPress.

I'm looking for some feedback from industry pros—not trying to sell you anything right now. Would you be open to a 5-min 'Inventory Health Check' next week?

I’d love to see if our logic matches how you're currently handling your cutting room floor. Best, [Your Name]"

Mind if I send the link?

Cheers,
[Your Name]"
